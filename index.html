Cascade Pump Curve Digitizer

Drop a pump curve here

Drag in a PNG or JPG, paste one from the clipboard, or choose a file. Calibrate the flow and TDH axes by clicking two ticks on each, and the head curves are traced for you to check and adjust.

Digitizes head (TDH) against flow for one or several speeds or impeller sizes, and hydraulic efficiency against flow, either from efficiency lines (click where each one crosses the head curve and type its value) or from a separate efficiency curve. Data copies to Excel or downloads as CSV. Power and NPSH curves are not digitized.
TDH points Click a point to select it, then nudge it with the arrow keys (Shift moves 5 px). Drag to move, double-click to remove.
